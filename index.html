Process

A four-stage journey from first call to agreed HMRC plan.

Step 1

Calm, confidential review

Discuss arrears, deadlines and current HMRC contact without judgement, to understand the real starting point.

Step 2

Affordability & commercial reality

Explore income, essential costs and commitments to identify payment levels that can genuinely be sustained.

Step 3

Proposal structure & evidence

Prepare the narrative, figures and explanations needed before any HMRC Time to Pay request is made.

Step 4

Ongoing support & adjustments

Stay engaged so clients don’t feel left alone once a plan is agreed, especially if circumstances change.
