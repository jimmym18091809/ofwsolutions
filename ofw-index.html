Why specialist HMRC support matters

HMRC helps genuine cases, but expects clear evidence, realistic proposals and prompt communication. We sit between you and HMRC to make that happen.

Stop enforcement before it escalates

HMRC uses letters, phone calls, debt collection agencies and court action when arrears are ignored. Early, structured engagement can prevent distraint, winding-up petitions and personal liability exposure.

Turn confusion into a clear plan

Most clients feel overwhelmed by tax codes, penalties and deadlines. We translate HMRC requirements into plain English and design a repayment plan around real-world cash flow.

Protect both business and personal position

Many directors face a mix of PAYE, VAT, Corporation Tax and Self Assessment debts. We coordinate across tax heads to minimise risk and keep your business trading where viable.

How we work with you

A structured, four-step process designed to move you from anxiety and uncertainty to an agreed HMRC plan.

1

Confidential discovery call

We review your situation, tax heads, arrears, deadlines and enforcement stage. No jargon, no judgment – just a clear picture of what HMRC sees.

2

Affordability and cash-flow assessment

We analyse income, essential costs and existing commitments to identify a realistic monthly payment HMRC is likely to accept.

3

Proposal preparation & evidence

We prepare the figures, forecasts and explanations HMRC expects, aligned with its Time to Pay guidance, and agree the negotiation strategy.

4

HMRC negotiation & follow-through

We engage HMRC debt management teams on your behalf where appropriate, and stay involved until the arrangement is agreed and documented.
